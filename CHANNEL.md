# Channel text (About pages)

Paste these into YouTube Studio → Customisation → Basic info → Description (limit 1,000 characters each). Both channels use the same layout: a short hook, the schedule, a call to swipe, then the sign-off line.

## Tower Clash (@TowerClashShorts)

Channel name: **TOWER CLASH**

Description:

```
TOWER CLASH: a brand-new tower-defence battle every 2 hours. Enemy armies march down the lanes and the towers fight back: archers, cannons, frost witches, dragons and more. Watch a defence hold or break in under a minute, with sound, in 4K.

New Shorts 12 times a day, on the even hours (London time).
Swipe for the next battle.

GOLD v PURPLE. Every tower. Every wave.
```

## Army Clash

ARMY CLASH: a brand-new battle every 2 hours. Red v Blue, armies of giants, dragons and titans clash on a different map every time, with sound, in 4K. Each battle ends in under a minute.

## Art

Both channels use the banner and profile picture made by `node brand.js` (files in `out/`): the same ray burst, the same troop placement and the same name font and size. The Tower Clash theme is violet; Army Clash keeps sky blue.
